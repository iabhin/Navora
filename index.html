About

One desk, from the shortlist to the visa file.

NAVORA is a counselling desk, not a portal. There is no account and no hand-off to a different person. The counsellor who reads your enquiry is the one who replies on WhatsApp. The work is a shortlist that fits the marks and the intake, the papers each university names, a reading of an offer before it is accepted, and the visa file that follows on that same thread.

Counselling is 100% free. An offer is never promised. If the course is a poor fit, that is said before an application is filed. A university’s own application fee, when it has one, is named before anything is sent.

Shortlist
Countries and courses that fit the marks, the budget, and an intake you can make.
Documents
Transcripts, statements, and forms, named the way the university names them.
Offers
Conditions, deposits, and dates, read before you accept.
Visa file
The papers that follow an offer, still on the same thread.

Students

Who writes to the desk.

Most enquiries sound like one of these. If yours does not, use the form or WhatsApp.

  1. 01

    A first degree

    Board results are in, and the course list is longer than the countries you have looked at. The first job is to cut that list before any application fee is paid.

  2. 02

    A master’s after some work

    You have been out of class for a year or two. The useful questions are the intake, the documents you still have, and whether the move is worth the course.

  3. 03

    A parent on the phone

    The student is not always the person who calls. We still need their marks, their passport timing, and a number that reaches them.

  4. 04

    A plan that changed

    An offer arrived with conditions, or a date moved. The desk stays on the existing file instead of starting again in a new chat.
